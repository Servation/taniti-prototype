Getting here and around

Taniti offers several transportation options: public buses in Taniti City, island-wide private buses, local taxis, rental cars near the airport, and bicycle rentals.

Arriving on Taniti

The airport accommodates small jets and propeller planes. Some visitors arrive on a small cruise ship that docks at Yellow Leaf Bay for one night per week.

Public & private buses

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island.

Taxis & rental cars

Taxis are available in Taniti City. Rental cars are available from a local rental agency near the airport.

Walking & cycling

Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable. Merriton Landing is easy to explore on foot. Bikes and helmets are available to rent; helmets are required.
