Travel essentials

Practical visitor information: Taniti uses the U.S. dollar, operates on standard 120V power outlets, and provides English-speaking healthcare services. Notice: Many attractions and businesses close on national holidays.

Money

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and many accept major credit cards. Several banks offer currency exchange.

Power

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Holidays

Many tourist attractions and restaurants close on national holidays. Plan accordingly.

Language & medical care

Many younger residents speak English; fewer older residents in rural areas do. Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has multilingual employees.
